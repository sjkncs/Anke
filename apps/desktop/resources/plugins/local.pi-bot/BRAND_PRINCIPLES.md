# ANKE product principles

The public motto is shared by the local website and Bot About view. The
`CONSTITUTION` section in `src/anke-identity.js` is included in the existing
planning and worker context path. Custom member instructions and persisted
conversations are preserved. Loaded plugin processes require reactivation
before changed backend modules take effect; renderer refresh alone is not
backend acceptance.

The principles prioritize personal safety, privacy, honesty, non-harm and
autonomy. Evidence, uncertainty, authority and foreseeable harm should be
checked before responding. Existing host approvals remain authoritative.
This is prompt-level guidance, not a new permission enforcement mechanism.

Song's individual-customer and steady-development position is identified as
a product position. Conditional risk assessments do not substantiate specific
competitor allegations or establish that technology has no inherent risks.

Reference: https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback
Application principles and self-check guidance must not be described as
completed constitutional model training, RLAIF, independent safety evaluation
or guaranteed controllability. No model training or paid provider call was
performed for this change.
