---
name: household-affordability
description: Calculate household monthly housing expenditure and income burden.
---
Confirm area, monthly rent, separately charged property fees and household income.
Compute monthly expenditure = rent + separately paid fees, then divide by income.
Report rent/income separately from all-in housing expenditure/income. The latter
is an extended household service measure, not automatically the manuscript's
rent burden definition. State whether income is disposable income and never
silently change its definition. Burden is one evaluation dimension of the thesis,
not a replacement for estimating the quality-adjusted market rent benchmark.
Distinguish unit rent from monthly household expense. Ask for missing inputs.
For adjustment scenarios confirm reference rent, subsidy ratio and increase cap.
Present parameter sources and calculations without asserting an unverified policy.
